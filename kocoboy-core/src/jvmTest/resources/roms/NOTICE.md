These `.gb` test ROMs are prebuilt binaries used here as regression
fixtures for `kocoboy-core` emulation.

## mooneye/

From the [Mooneye Test Suite](https://github.com/Gekkio/mooneye-test-suite)
by Joonas Javanainen, licensed under the MIT License. Results have been
verified against real hardware by the mooneye-test-suite authors.

## blargg/

From [Blargg's Game Boy test ROMs](https://github.com/retrio/gb-test-roms)
by Shay Green ("Blargg"). No explicit license is published for these ROMs;
they are, however, widely mirrored and embedded directly (as prebuilt
binaries) by numerous other open-source Game Boy emulator projects which is
the precedent followed here.

## dmg-acid2/

[dmg-acid2](https://github.com/mattcurrie/dmg-acid2) by Matt Currie, licensed
under the MIT License. `reference-dmg.png` is the project's reference image of
the correct output.
